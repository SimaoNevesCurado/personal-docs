## #why-public

I use this to organize what I discover, keep useful commands close and document patterns I do not want to lose.

Some things here may be incomplete, outdated or conceptually wrong. If you find something I can improve, I would be happy if you contacted me.

It is public so I can access it whenever I need it, and so it may help people who are starting out. Official documentation can be excellent, but not always straight forward when you are still connecting the dots.
